1. Data We Collect

Watslink is designed with privacy as a core principle. We collect minimal data, and importantly, we do not store any phone numbers, messages, or generated links on our servers.

Data processed locally in your browser:

  • Phone number — Used only to generate the WhatsApp link. Never transmitted to our servers.
  • Prefilled message — Used only to generate the WhatsApp link with the text parameter. Never stored.
  • Custom link slug — Used only to display the formatted short link preview. Not stored unless you use a third-party URL shortener.
  • Country selection — Used to format the phone number correctly with the appropriate dial code.

Data we do not collect:

  • Personal identifiers (name, email, IP address)
  • Generated WhatsApp links or QR codes
  • Usage analytics or tracking data
  • Device fingerprinting information

2. How We Use Data

All data processing happens entirely in your browser (client-side). The data you enter is used solely to:

  • Generate a valid WhatsApp wa.me link with the correct phone number format
  • URL-encode your prefilled message for the text parameter
  • Generate a QR code representing the WhatsApp link
  • Display a preview of what your custom short link would look like

No data leaves your device during normal operation. The generated WhatsApp link opens directly in WhatsApp when clicked — we do not proxy, track, or log these clicks.

3. Data Storage & Security

Local Storage

We may use your browser's localStorage to remember your preferences:

  • Last selected country code
  • Custom link toggle preference

This data never leaves your device and can be cleared at any time by clearing your browser data.

No Server-Side Storage

We do not operate a database. We do not log, archive, or retain any user-submitted data. The application is a static site hosted on a CDN with no backend processing.

HTTPS & Security Headers

All connections use HTTPS. We implement security headers including:

  • Content Security Policy (CSP)
  • X-Frame-Options: DENY
  • X-Content-Type-Options: nosniff
  • Referrer-Policy: strict-origin-when-cross-origin

4. Third-Party Services

Watslink loads the following third-party resources:

  • Google Fonts (fonts.googleapis.com) — For DM Sans and Space Grotesk fonts. Google's privacy policy applies.
  • QRCode.js (cdn.jsdelivr.net) — For QR code generation. Loaded from jsDelivr CDN.

These services may collect standard request data (IP address, user agent) as part of their normal CDN operations. We do not share any of your input data with these services.

Important: If you choose to use a third-party URL shortener (Bitly, Rebrandly, etc.) to make your custom short links functional, those services have their own privacy policies and data practices. Watslink is not responsible for their data handling.

5. Cookies & Local Storage

Watslink does not use cookies for tracking, analytics, or advertising.

We use localStorage only for the preferences mentioned above. You can view, modify, or delete this data at any time through your browser's developer tools (Application → Local Storage).

6. Your Rights

Since we don't collect personal data, there's no personal data to access, rectify, or delete. However, you have full control over:

  • Local data: Clear browser data to remove any stored preferences
  • Generated links: Links are generated in your browser — you control where they're shared
  • QR codes: Generated locally and can be downloaded or discarded

7. Children's Privacy

Watslink is not directed at children under 13 (or 16 in some jurisdictions). We do not knowingly collect personal information from children. Since we collect no personal information at all, this is inherently satisfied.

8. Changes to This Policy

We may update this Privacy Policy to reflect changes in our practices or legal requirements. The "Last updated" date at the top indicates the most recent revision. Continued use of Watslink after changes constitutes acceptance of the updated policy.

For material changes, we'll provide notice through the application interface.

9. Contact Us

If you have questions about this Privacy Policy or Watslink's privacy practices, please contact us:

  • Email: privacy@watslink.io
  • Website: watslink.io

We typically respond within 5 business days.
