Frequently Asked Questions

When you create a custom short link (e.g., watslink.io/your-brand), we store the mapping between your slug and the full WhatsApp link in our database. When someone visits watslink.io/your-brand, our server looks up the slug and redirects them to the full WhatsApp link. This happens server-side, so it works everywhere — social media, QR codes, emails, anywhere.

Yes! The links work with both regular WhatsApp and WhatsApp Business accounts. If you have a WhatsApp Business account, you can also use the business parameter for catalog links, but our generator focuses on the standard chat functionality.

WhatsApp doesn't enforce a strict character limit on the text parameter, but URLs have a practical limit of ~2000 characters. We limit the message to 2000 characters to ensure compatibility. For longer content, consider linking to a webpage instead.

Currently, click tracking is not built into the free version. For analytics, you can use UTM parameters in a landing page that redirects to WhatsApp, or integrate with a URL shortener service that provides analytics (Bitly, Rebrandly, etc.).

Yes! The links work on WhatsApp Web, WhatsApp Desktop, and mobile apps. On desktop, clicking the link opens the web version or prompts to open the desktop app.

Since this is a free, client-side generator without accounts, short links are stored in a shared database. If you need guaranteed ownership and management of your short links, we recommend using a dedicated URL shortener service (Bitly, Rebrandly) with your own custom domain.
